# Third-party model notice

The files in `public/models/` are anatomical assets derived from open datasets:

Exception: `body-context.glb` is a locally authored illustrative mannequin,
generated by `scripts/build-body-context.cjs`. It is not a Z-Anatomy/BodyParts3D
skin model, a patient surface, or validated anatomical geometry. It provides
approximate body context for the visceral layer without altering the organs.

- `esqueleto-anatomico.glb` is the skeletal-system layer.
- `musculos.glb` is the muscular/tendon layer distributed with BodyExplorer.
- `cardiovascular.glb`, `nervoso.glb`, and `orgaos.glb` are the cardiovascular,
  nervous/sense-organ, and visceral layers exported from Z-Anatomy.
  Conversion source: https://github.com/Liyucheng1997/242_lab-human-anatomy
  Revision: `322ba39e96ea91ce08601caad2c0aaade1dc99f0`.
  Source filenames: `cardiovascular.glb`, `nervous.glb`, `visceral.glb`.
  These three files are distributed unchanged, with Z-Anatomy attribution
  (Gauthier Kervyn and contributors), under CC BY-SA 4.0.

These models derive from Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D materials. BodyExplorer identifies
its BodyParts3D data as © The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan

as CC BY-SA 4.0. Preserve those respective attributions and ShareAlike terms.

- Skeletal source project: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
- Skeletal GLB conversion source: https://github.com/Liyucheng1997/242_lab-human-anatomy

Changes made by this application are limited to runtime grouping, materials,
lighting, articulation pivots, visibility, highlighting, and schematic separation
of structures. The source GLB is unchanged. Redistributions of the model or adaptations
must preserve attribution and the respective ShareAlike license terms above.

The new catalogs retain original source labels. Explicit Portuguese translations
are editorial and await specialist review; untranslated names remain in English.
The visceral dataset represents male anatomy and is not a complete inventory of
every organ. Preview availability does not imply clinical validation.

## Homepage study plate

`public/images/skeleton-study.webp` is a static rendering of the supplied skeletal
GLB, derived from Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D (Gauthier Kervyn and contributors;
The Database Center for Life Science). It preserves the model's geometry and
uses custom lighting, materials and framing. The image retains the source
attributions and respective ShareAlike terms listed above. Reproduce it with
`scripts/render-skeleton-study.cjs` (Node.js, Three.js and Python with Pillow).
